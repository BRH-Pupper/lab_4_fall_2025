PUPPER DEBUG NOTES - Sunday Oct 25, 2026
==========================================

TL;DR
-----
- Original problem: in trot, the front-left leg wasn't working well and the
  pupper kept turning instead of trotting straight.
- Trotting at 3.6 Hz (gait-change branch) fixed that: it walked in a straight
  line. But the Raspberry Pi browned out and shut down.
- The brownout is NOT caused by our code. It now happens on the ground with
  every version we tried, even at 2.4 Hz and 2.0 Hz, including code that
  walked fine for a long time in September.
- Most likely cause: the Pi's 5V power path (5V converter, its wiring /
  connectors, or a ground shared with the motors). The battery is fine: the
  motors keep holding position while the Pi dies.
- Next step: power the Pi from a separate USB-C supply and walk it on the
  ground. If the brownouts stop, fix/replace the 5V converter and wiring.


1. WHAT WE SAW
--------------
- Pi logs "Undervoltage detected!" while walking, then sometimes loses power
  completely and reboots.
- The motors keep holding position when the Pi dies, so the 20V battery side
  stays up. Only the Pi's 5V supply drops.
- The Pi's 5V rail sits at ~4.84V even when idle (a Pi 5 wants 5.1V; it
  flags undervoltage at ~4.63V). Very little margin.
- One undervoltage even happened while the Pi was booting, with no motors
  running.
- History: 1 undervoltage event on Sep 20, then none until today (8+ today).

4. OTHER FINDINGS
-----------------
a) IMU "failed to read header" messages
   - Means the Pi asked the IMU for data over I2C and it never answered.
   - The kernel shows "SDA stuck at low": the IMU jams the I2C data wire,
     about 5 s into walking, BEFORE the Pi's undervoltage alarm.
   - The battery-voltage sensor shares that I2C bus, so it also stops
     answering when the bus is jammed.
   - Doesn't affect the gait (lab_4.py doesn't use the IMU).
   - To clear it: power off the whole robot at the battery.
   - IMU dropouts have happened on and off since July (18 launches).

b) Gait "lagging" 2-3 times during the 2-minute stand run
   - Not the cache filling up (it's a fixed 50-frame loop).
   - The gait loop in lab_4.py stalled 30-45 ms at a time, in 4 bursts.
   - Likely cause: main logs a big debug line on EVERY 120 Hz tick
     (~80 KB/s to the terminal and to the log file).
   - Fix (not yet tested on the robot): log once per gait cycle. Saved in
     git stash, see section 6.

c) Clue for the loose wire
   - In the run where the Pi lost power, all 12 motors' readings froze at
     the same time 18.5% of the time. That points to a SHARED wire (power,
     ground, or the Pi-to-motor-board link), not one motor.
   - Mild outlier: leg_back_r_1 (back-right hip side-swing) runs 5-10%
     behind its left twin in every run. Worth wiggling its connector.

d) About the original front-left leg / turning issue
   - At 3.6 Hz it trotted straight. We haven't yet checked why the
     front-left leg misbehaved at slower speeds, because the brownouts got
     in the way. Revisit once power is fixed.
